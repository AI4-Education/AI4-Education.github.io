Team capabilities

People-centred by design.

These complementary roles describe the expertise needed to build and evaluate responsible educational innovation.

Capability profile01

Education & Pedagogy Lead

Connects the initiative to curriculum, teaching practice, classroom realities and educator development.

Focus: Curriculum design · Teaching practice · Teacher engagement

Capability profile02

AI & Technology Lead

Evaluates AI capabilities, develops prototypes and reviews reliability, security and system limitations.

Focus: Prototyping · Technical evaluation · Integration

Capability profile03

Research & Evaluation Lead

Shapes research questions, methods and robust evaluation of learning outcomes and experiences.

Focus: Study design · Mixed methods · Evidence synthesis

Capability profile04

Responsible AI & Governance Lead

Supports privacy, fairness, transparency, accessibility, safeguarding and proportionate governance.

Focus: Ethics · Privacy · Risk review

Capability profile05

Partnerships & Community Lead

Builds relationships with schools, learners, universities and community organisations for collaborative work.

Focus: Co-design · Partnerships · Community engagement

Capability profile06

Communications & Resources Lead

Translates complex findings into accessible resources, events and practical guidance.

Focus: Publishing · Events · Knowledge sharing

Student and educator voices are essential.

We aim to involve learners and teachers in identifying needs, shaping prototypes and interpreting outcomes—rather than treating them only as end users.
