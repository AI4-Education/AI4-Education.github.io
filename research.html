Research

Research questions that matter for learning.

Six connected themes guide our inquiry into effective, inclusive and responsible AI in education. These are areas of investigation, not claims of completed research.

Home/Research

Research agenda

Evidence before assumptions.

Our approach brings educational practice, technical evaluation and ethical reflection together.

Research theme01

AI-augmented teaching

How can AI support planning, explanation, feedback and differentiated materials while preserving teacher expertise and relationships?

Areas of inquiry

Teacher review workflows; output verification; professional learning needs.

Research theme02

Personalised learning

When can adaptive prompts and scaffolds help students learn at their own pace without reducing agency or reinforcing inequity?

Areas of inquiry

Learner control; accessibility; data minimisation; measurable progress.

Research theme03

AI literacy and critical thinking

What knowledge and habits help learners understand generative AI, check its claims and recognise bias or fabricated information?

Areas of inquiry

Age-appropriate AI literacy; source evaluation; responsible disclosure.

Research theme04

Ethics, safety and inclusion

How should schools address privacy, consent, fairness, accessibility, safeguarding and academic integrity when using AI?

Areas of inquiry

Risk assessment; transparency; inclusive co-design; governance.

Research theme05

Learning outcomes and evaluation

Does AI assistance improve understanding, retention, motivation and participation—or merely accelerate task completion?

Areas of inquiry

Mixed-methods evaluation; baseline comparisons; unintended effects.

Research theme06

Education futures and assessment

How might AI change curriculum, authentic assessment, teacher roles and the capabilities learners will need?

Areas of inquiry

Assessment redesign; human skills; equitable access; policy scenarios.

✳

Research approach: We envisage literature reviews, participatory co-design, classroom-informed inquiry and careful evaluation. Studies involving participants would require appropriate ethics, consent, privacy and safeguarding processes.
