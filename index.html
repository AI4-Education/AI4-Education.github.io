Welcome to AI4Education

Where education meets possibility.

AI4Education is a collaborative initiative exploring how artificial intelligence can enrich teaching and learning without losing sight of people, evidence, equity or educational purpose. We connect research questions with classroom needs, practical prototypes and shared learning.
