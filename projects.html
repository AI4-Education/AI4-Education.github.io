Proposed portfolio

From questions to prototypes.

Each concept starts with an educational challenge and a plan to test its value responsibly.

Student learning01

AI Learning Companion

A guided study-support concept for secondary learners that offers hints, retrieval practice and reflection instead of simply supplying answers.

Proposed next steps

Prototype Socratic prompts; trial curriculum-aligned questions; review accuracy and age-appropriate safeguards.

Intended outcome: More independent practice and stronger metacognition.

Educator support02

Teacher Resource Studio

A teacher-controlled workflow for drafting, adapting and checking lesson materials, exercises and feedback examples.

Proposed next steps

Co-design templates with educators; evaluate quality, workload and accessibility; keep final approval with teachers.

Intended outcome: Useful preparation support without delegating professional judgement.

AI literacy03

AI Literacy for Schools

A modular set of classroom activities that explain how generative AI works and why its outputs require critical evaluation.

Proposed next steps

Create bias and hallucination investigations; teach verification; develop clear AI-use acknowledgement practices.

Intended outcome: Confident, critical and responsible AI use.

Assessment04

Assessment in an AI-enabled World

A co-design initiative exploring tasks that assess reasoning, process, application and authentic understanding.

Proposed next steps

Test process portfolios, oral explanations, reflective logs and explicit permitted-use rules.

Intended outcome: Assessment practices that remain meaningful when AI tools are widely available.

Inclusion05

Accessible Learning with AI

An exploration of translation, reading-level adaptation, speech and text supports, developed with accessibility expertise.

Proposed next steps

Evaluate usefulness and error rates; involve diverse learners; assess privacy and consent.

Intended outcome: Reduce barriers without replacing specialist support.

Evaluation06

Evidence of Impact

A reusable framework for measuring educational outcomes and risks of AI-supported interventions.

Proposed next steps

Define outcomes and baselines; collect learner and teacher perspectives; report limitations.

Intended outcome: Decisions grounded in evidence rather than novelty.

✳

Project status: All six entries are proposed concepts. As projects are approved, this page can add verified partners, timelines, milestones, outputs and evaluation findings.
